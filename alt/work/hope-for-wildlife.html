Hope for Wildlife.

Every animal has a story. Every rescue offers a chance.

Genre
Wildlife
Produced by
Arcadia Entertainment
Catalogue
Part of roughly 500 hours of factual programming

At a wildlife rehabilitation centre in Nova Scotia, Hope Swinimer and her team care for injured and orphaned wild animals, working toward the moment they can return them to the wild.

Moving, funny and unpredictable, Hope for Wildlife follows the patients and the people who refuse to give up on them.
