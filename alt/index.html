To the people saving wild lives. The machines doing extraordinary things. The mysteries beneath the surface. And the unexpected stories behind the world we live in.

Arcadia is an independent factual production company based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. For 25 years, we’ve followed our curiosity around the world, creating documentaries and series that entertain, enlighten and surprise.

01 Our story

Curiosity takes us places.

Sometimes to the bottom of the ocean. Sometimes inside an extraordinary machine. Sometimes through the door of a wildlife hospital, where a small animal’s survival becomes the biggest story in the room.

Arcadia began in Halifax in 2001. Since then, we’ve made hundreds of hours of factual television, following people with unusual ambitions, exploring places few get to see, and asking questions that open up entire worlds.

Our work spans wildlife, adventure, history, science and engineering. What connects it is a belief that the real world is endlessly entertaining, if you know where to look.

03 What we do

Extraordinary stories. Lasting possibilities.

Arcadia creates, owns and develops factual entertainment.

Based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, we bring 25 years of production experience to a growing catalogue of documentaries and series spanning wildlife, adventure, history, science and engineering.

Today, that means original productions, international partnerships, new programmes drawn from existing footage, and opportunities in digital distribution and artificial intelligence.

05 What’s next

Still curious. Just getting started.

Our next stories take us into new territory: the unexpected origins of modern life, the human stories behind extraordinary events, and the decisions that change everything.

We’re developing new series and formats with collaborators who share our appetite for discovery, strong characters and stories that keep audiences watching.
